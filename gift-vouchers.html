Good to know

Questions

Where can I use the voucher?

In the café on St Helens Road, against anything on the menu — eat in or take away. Ask us if you'd like to use it towards platters, the party room or the ice cream bike.

Can you post it to me?

Yes. Ring us with the amount and the address and we'll pop it in the post the same day.

Is everything halal?

Yes — the kitchen is halal by default. Vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free swaps are no extra charge.

Can I gift a platter instead?

You can. Platters start at £15 and need 72 hours' notice, so we can plan the ingredients and deliver.

Do you deliver?

The café menu is on Uber Eats and Deliveroo, and platter orders are delivered free within two miles. Delivery apps charge us a platform fee, so their prices are a little higher than in the café — the menu here is the in-café price.

Full terms are printed on each voucher and available in the café.
