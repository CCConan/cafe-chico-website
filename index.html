Our story

We treat every plate like we mean it.

Café Chico started with a family decision: bring proper, honest food to our neighbourhood. We upcycled the seating from an old church, grew the herbs we cook with, and slowly built a menu around breakfast and brunch that we'd want to eat ourselves.

Three years on, the chairs still creak in the same spot. The food still comes out hot, every time. And the regulars still come in like they're walking into a friend's front room.

"Small and cosy place, the food is delicious. You can feel they actually care."

— Aliyah · 5★ Google review
