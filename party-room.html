The layout

A room for twelve.
Set up your way.

The room is roughly 6m × 5m. The long table sits 8 comfortably, 12 at a push. The window wall faces St Helens Rd. The heart-wreath is on the back wall.

What you get

Everything in the room is yours for the booking.

The room itself

Wooden table and chairs for up to 12, set up however you'd like. The heart-wreath wall. The view onto St Helens Rd.

Free WiFi

Fast broadband. Good for work meetings, laptop days, planning sessions. The WiFi password is on the wall.

Plates & cutlery

If you're bringing food or having a cake, we can set out plates, forks, knives, napkins. Just ask when you book.

Tea & coffee station

Unlimited tea and coffee from the café downstairs. Plus hot chocolate if anyone wants it. We bring it up.

Bluetooth speaker

One speaker in the room, already paired. Bring a playlist, hit play. No need to set up anything.

Café menu downstairs

Your guests can come down to the counter to order food and drinks — no need to come through your booking. Or pre-order platters (see platters page).

What it's good for

Twelve people. A handful of ideas.

  • Birthday parties

    Especially kids' birthdays. The café downstairs keeps a regular flow; upstairs you get your own corner of noise and cake.

  • Baby showers & hen dos

    Bring your own decorations, your own drinks, your own playlist. The room's the canvas.

  • Small business meetings

    Up to 12 people. Big table, fast WiFi, tea and coffee always on. The café downstairs handles lunch if you need it.

  • Workshops & classes

    Book club, craft circle, planning committee — whatever needs a regular slot and a quiet space.

  • Small wakes & remembrance

    Quiet, private, easy to access. We can put on tea, coffee, and a platter of sandwiches if you need it.

What to know before you book

  • Capacity — 12 people max. If you're more, ask about the café-after-hours option.
  • Access — one flight of stairs, no lift. Tell us if anyone has mobility needs and we'll figure something out downstairs.
  • Noise — the café is open 9–4 Tue–Sun; we can be flexible outside those hours with notice.
  • Food & drink — you can bring your own food and (non-alcoholic) drink. Or order from our menu / platters.
  • Decorations — go for it. Blu-Tack and pins are fine, please no glitter (it gets in the cake).
  • Cancellation — full refund up to 7 days before, 50% within 7 days, no refund within 48 hours.
